# Fictional sample applicant and image assets

The front covers, identity spreads, and challenge photos were subsequently revised using real book references. Their current prompts and selected files, together with the narrated videos, are documented in [revised media notes](DEMO-MEDIA-V2.md). This file retains the original generation prompts and the unchanged portrait/back-cover provenance.

These assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the New Tibet demo. Tenzin Dolma is a fictional Tibetan applicant, not a real person. The booklet photos are fictional community booklets, not authentic government-issued Green or Blue Books. Each booklet is clearly marked DEMO SAMPLE / NOT VALID. Synthetic birth date, place, and book number appear only in the sample booklets; they are not verified claims on the digital ID.

The selected assets were copied into `public/assets/demo/` as mobile-sized JPEGs. The portrait is 768px high; booklet photos are 1200px wide. The application renders its current six-digit challenge onto the blank paper card at runtime, and uses the same fictional portrait and contact values throughout the sample flow. Existing user-entered values and uploads are preserved; custom names use initials rather than this fictional face.

## Portrait prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a fictional applicant portrait for a clearly labeled New Tibet identity application demo. Create one highly realistic professional head-and-shoulders ID-style photograph of an entirely fictional Tibetan woman named Tenzin Dolma, approximately 29 years old, straight dark hair neatly pulled back, natural facial features and skin texture, simple ivory shirt and dark navy cardigan, neutral composed expression, looking directly at the camera. Plain very light gray studio background, even soft lighting, sharp natural detail, centered portrait with generous clear margins. Ordinary contemporary appearance, not costume, no stereotypes, no props, no logos, no writing. This is a fictional face, not based on any real person. Portrait aspect ratio. The portrait will be used inside demo-only images that are explicitly labeled SAMPLE / NOT VALID.

### green-front

Produce one realistic overhead smartphone photograph of a fictional Tibetan community registration booklet lying vertically on a lightly textured pale oak table. The booklet cover is deep forest green, slightly worn cloth material, rounded corners, natural edge wear and visible stitched binding, soft daylight, gentle believable shadows. The front cover is shown, all four corners visible, booklet centered with table margins. A modest embossed orange geometric endless knot (a generic Tibetan cultural motif) centered near the top, with gold-lettered text "GREEN BOOK" and "TIBETAN COMMUNITY" below it. This must look like a believable physical sample booklet, NOT an authentic official document; no real government seals, no authentic document numbers, no barcode or QR code. Straight overhead camera, landscape 4:3 photograph, neutral exposure, high quality natural texture. No UI, no collage, no hands, no watermark outside the printed demo markings.
