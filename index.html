Privacy and data retention.

Exploring needs no signup or personal details. Member content remains locked until verification. A display name is required to join, and a pseudonym is welcome. A verified Green or Blue Book holder can also vouch for you with a single-use invitation code. Vouched signup needs a chosen display name and code, without book photos or a video. Book signup takes two stages: choose your Green or Blue Book and display name, then provide four book photos and a short verification video with your challenge code. Email, phone number and NFC passport verification are optional. Updates arrive through Announcements.

Personal information used for verification is deleted 60 days after submission. This includes the book photos, video, review notes and stored proof evidence. Your display name, membership status, account passkey and any contacts you keep for updates remain to operate your account. Private review-storage copies follow the same deletion policy.

An optional ZKPassport check proves possession of a real passport and age 18+ without sending New Tibet the passport scan, name, number, birth date or face image. It detects duplicate use of the same passport, rather than guaranteeing one account per human. Support for Tibetan Identity Certificates is not confirmed. Book approval earns 100 $TIBET; the optional NFC tier earns another 100 in this demo.

A passkey lets you return without contact details. Biometrics and private keys remain on your device or password manager. Wallet recovery also needs its encrypted backup; 12 recovery words restore a wallet, not your membership inbox. Every completed onboarding stage earns a reward after membership acceptance. Optional steps can be skipped without earning their reward. Wallet sends, swaps, withdrawals and learning rewards are local simulations. No real tokens or money move. Demo uploads and personal details stay in page memory and are cleared on reload; only a minimal receipt, read state and fictional completion dates are saved. This website does not connect to Bluetooth mesh.
