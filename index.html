YOUR INFORMATION

How we use your information.

A display name is required, and you can use a pseudonym instead of your real name. This name is displayed in the app. Email and phone number are optional; you can receive updates through Announcements without sharing them. Book verification still requires four book photos, a challenge code, video, and a private passport proof. Those book photos and video may contain personally identifiable information. New Tibet uses your chosen display name for your profile. Any optional contacts are used for verification and optional WhatsApp updates. A passkey stores a public key for returning to your account. Only authorized reviewers can access your documents and video. These are separate from the private passport check.

The ZKPassport check stores cryptographic proofs, a private identifier that prevents duplicate passport use. New Tibet does not receive or store your passport scan, passport name, passport number, nationality, date of birth, or passport face image. ZKPassport performs the face check on your phone. The age check proves age 18 or older without disclosing your date of birth. Duplicate-passport protection cannot guarantee one account per human across multiple passports.

Codes verify any optional contacts you add. A manual review determines identity approval. Your application receipt, review decisions, and important New Tibet announcements are available in a one-way in-app inbox; there is no reply or posting function for members. Updates refresh while the app is open. A verified WhatsApp number also receives application messages. A passkey lets you return without providing contact details.

The profile card can show a QR code for membership checks at participating physical locations. A live check discloses only the member reference, book type, current approval status and card validity. Apple and Google Wallet membership passes use the member reference and exclude names, contacts, portraits and passport data. Anyone with a copy of the QR can check those membership details; it does not let them sign into your account. Demo QR codes open a clearly marked preview and do not verify real membership. Saving a real pass requires New Tibet to enable its wallet issuer.

A passkey keeps its private key in your device or password manager. Only its public key is stored by New Tibet. The demo can generate an Ethereum wallet address. A supported passkey encrypts its recovery material locally; the passkey encryption output never goes to New Tibet. Alternatively, you can record 12 recovery words. Words are cleared from the page after backup confirmation or when you leave wallet setup. Demo wallet material is held only in this page’s memory and is lost on reload. Demo balances, swaps, and bank withdrawals are simulations. No token is issued and no money is moved. Do not send funds to this demo wallet.

In test mode, book photos, videos, and contacts stay in this page’s memory and are cleared on reload. A ZKPassport request connects to its service if you choose to start it, but the preview has no backend to verify or store returned proofs. No messages are sent. Sample photos depict an AI-generated fictional applicant. Sample petition results, created petitions, chat messages, and wallet activity stay in this page’s memory. The demo saves only your application reference, book type, status history, and inbox read state on this browser so the record survives reload. It does not save your name, contacts, uploads, proofs, or wallet keys. Restart demo clears the saved receipt. In live mode the backend stores your inbox records; a temporary sign-in token is kept in this tab’s session storage to support reload, and a passkey can reopen the account after the session ends. Sample videos use a narrated montage of the fictional applicant’s photos. This website does not connect to BitChat’s native Bluetooth mesh. Use sample book photos and videos in this preview.

Contact hello@newtibet.com about your application or deletion. The operator must set a retention period and publish the final privacy notice before live collection.
