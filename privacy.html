DATA POLICY · UPDATED 9 OCTOBER 2026

Verification data.
Deleted after 60 days.

Keep enough information to review an application. Keep less information once the review is complete.

Explore before joining

You can enter the app without signup, a display name or any verification evidence. Only an exploration preference is saved on this browser. All tabs are available, with member chats, wallet actions, ecosystem services and member announcements locked until membership verification. Petitions are readable; supporting or creating one requires verified Green Book membership. Application receipts remain visible while your review is pending.

Profile contains the signup and verification stages, a progress ladder, optional NFC verification, wallet security and optional contact details. Draft names and evidence stay in memory until submission and are not saved in the exploration preference.

What joining requires

A chosen display name, Green or Blue Book, four book photos and a short video with the challenge code. A display name can be a pseudonym. Photos and video can contain personal information and are available only to authorised reviewers.

Alternatively, a verified Green or Blue Book holder can vouch for you with an invitation code. You choose a display name and redeem the code without uploading a book or video. Live codes are single-use and expire after seven days. The account records which member vouched for you; that connection is private and not included in the public QR check or Wallet card. Vouched membership does not grant Green Book petition rights or the book-verification signup reward.

Every completed onboarding stage earns a demo reward once, after membership acceptance. Optional NFC, wallet security and contact verification can be skipped without earning that stage’s reward. Passport NFC verification, email and phone number are optional. Your application receipt, review decisions and New Tibet announcements are available in the app.

The 60-day verification policy

Personal information collected for verification is deleted 60 days after submission. This covers book photos and video in private storage, review notes, challenge evidence, and stored cryptographic proof evidence. Copies of this information in the review spreadsheet and application database are cleared too.

The backend schedules cleanup for new applications submitted under this policy. Failed storage cleanup is retried. The operator must apply the same limit to backups, exports and copies outside the application's control; prevent new backups from restoring erased evidence.

Your chosen display name, current membership status, account passkey public key and optional contacts retained for updates remain to operate your account. A private passport uniqueness identifier can remain to prevent repeated use of the same document. These are account records, not retained scans or video. Contact New Tibet to request account deletion or exercise your data rights.

Optional passport proof

ZKPassport performs an NFC chip and face check on the phone. New Tibet receives a cryptographic proof and a private duplicate-passport identifier, not your passport scan, name, number, birth date, nationality or face image. The proof can show age 18+ without revealing a birth date.

It checks the reuse of a passport; it does not establish one account per human across different documents. NFC support for Tibetan Identity Certificates is not confirmed, so this check is not required for basic membership.

Optional country-level totals

If you choose to contribute, your selected country of residence increments a country-level counter. The backend records that an account has contributed to prevent repeat counts, but does not keep the account's country. Public results suppress countries with fewer than 10 contributions. Passport nationality is not used to infer residence.

Do not log country selections with identity or contact details. Published totals can remain after an account is deleted because no per-account country is retained.

Account security and updates

Biometrics and passkey private keys stay on your device or password manager. New Tibet stores the public credential used for account sign-in. Wallet recovery also needs an encrypted backup; wallet recovery words do not restore a membership account.

Contact details are optional and must be verified if added. Email interest capture is separate from identity registration: email is used for New Tibet updates until you request removal. The live backend can record consent; the public demo neither saves that email nor sends it.

This public prototype

The GitHub Pages app is in demo mode. Fictional photos, videos, names and contact inputs stay in page memory and are cleared on reload. A minimal receipt, event and reward completion dates, reward read state and a fictional tier marker remain in browser storage until you restart the demo. No raw documents, personal contact details, proofs, wallet keys, invitation codes or recovery words are saved in that receipt. Demo invitation codes can be shared across browsers but do not authenticate the inviter or enforce single use across browsers. Only code fingerprints are kept locally to prevent reuse in the same browser.

Reward credits, campaigns, payments, sends, swaps and withdrawals are local demonstrations. No tokens or money move. This website does not connect to native Bluetooth mesh. Real wallet passes require configured Apple and Google issuers.

Rights and operation

Contact hello@newtibet.com for access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of optional consent. A live operator must publish its controller details and lawful basis, maintain processor agreements, restrict reviewers, protect storage and backups, and complete its data-protection assessment before collecting real verification evidence. This prototype does not certify GDPR compliance.

Reference: General Data Protection Regulation.
