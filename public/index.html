    YOUR INFORMATION

    Your identity stays private.

    In live mode, New Tibet collects your name, book photo, email, WhatsApp number, and passkey public key to process your application. Only authorized reviewers can access your documents.

    Email and WhatsApp codes establish access to your contacts. A manual review determines identity approval. Status messages go to your WhatsApp number.

    A passkey keeps its private key in your device or password manager. Only its public key is stored by New Tibet. This version does not create a crypto wallet or issue tokens.

    In test mode, documents and contacts stay in this page’s memory and are cleared on reload. No messages are sent. Do not use real identity documents in a demo.

    Contact hello@newtibet.com about your application or deletion. The operator must set a retention period and publish the final privacy notice before live collection.
