YOUR INFORMATION

How we use your information.

In live mode, New Tibet collects your name, four book photos, a challenge code, video message, email, WhatsApp number, and passkey public key to process your application. Only authorized reviewers can access your documents and video. These are separate from the private passport check.

The ZKPassport check stores cryptographic proofs, a private identifier that prevents duplicate passport use. New Tibet does not receive or store your passport scan, passport name, passport number, nationality, date of birth, or passport face image. ZKPassport performs the face check on your phone. The age check proves age 18 or older without disclosing your date of birth. Duplicate-passport protection cannot guarantee one account per human across multiple passports.

Email and WhatsApp codes establish access to your contacts. A manual review determines identity approval. Status messages go to your WhatsApp number.

A passkey keeps its private key in your device or password manager. Only its public key is stored by New Tibet. The demo can generate an Ethereum wallet address. A supported passkey encrypts its recovery material locally; the passkey encryption output never goes to New Tibet. Alternatively, you can record 12 recovery words. Words are cleared from the page after backup confirmation or when you leave wallet setup. Demo wallet material is held only in this page’s memory and is lost on reload. No token is issued and no transfer is enabled. Do not send funds to this demo wallet.

In test mode, book photos, videos, and contacts stay in this page’s memory and are cleared on reload. A ZKPassport request connects to its service if you choose to start it, but the preview has no backend to verify or store returned proofs. No messages are sent. Sample photos depict an AI-generated fictional applicant. Example petition signatures and chat messages stay in this page’s memory. This website does not connect to BitChat’s native Bluetooth mesh. Use sample book photos and videos in this preview.

Contact hello@newtibet.com about your application or deletion. The operator must set a retention period and publish the final privacy notice before live collection.
