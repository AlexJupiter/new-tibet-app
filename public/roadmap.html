Privacy by design

Two registration stages

Choose your book and display name, then provide verification evidence. A pseudonym is welcome; contacts and NFC verification are optional.

60-day deletion

Personal information collected for verification is deleted 60 days after submission. Membership and account-access records remain for the service.

Rewards with a purpose

230 free $TIBET

Every completed stage earns a reward: joining 10, book approval 100, optional passport NFC 100, security 10 and contact verification 10. Rewards are credited once after membership acceptance; optional stages can be skipped.

Participation rewards

Example: seven consecutive days of learning in Monlam or Dzongsar could earn 100 $TIBET. All campaigns and payouts here are demonstrations.

From application to community

Profile → Wallet → Chat → Petitions → Ecosystem. Announcements opens from the notification bell at the top right.

Petitions are readable by everyone; verified Green Book members can create and support them. Planned native BitChat chat uses nearby Bluetooth relays without a central messaging intermediary.

Possible ecosystem uses

Monlam premium services, Dzongsar learning rewards, smartvote Tibet sign-in, and payments at participating Tibet Houses or yoga courses are possibilities. No integration or venue agreement is claimed.
